# HAZE environment assets

These are a deliberately small subset of Kenney's packs. They are loaded locally by the game and are visual-only; gameplay collision remains generated in `public/index.html`.

- Source: [Kenney Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit)
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0). The included `kenney/nature/License.txt` is the pack license.
- Files used: selected trees, rocks, stump, log, bush, and grass GLB files.
- Source: [Kenney Graveyard Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit)
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0). The included `kenney/graveyard/License.txt` is the pack license.
- Files used: grave, broken gravestone, damaged fence, coffin, lantern, and the shared colormap texture.

The GLB assets are small, embedded-runtime models or use the local shared texture. No remote asset URL is used at runtime.
